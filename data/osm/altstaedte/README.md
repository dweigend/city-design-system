# Old-town OSM extracts

Data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The frozen extracts of Siena, Regensburg, Fès and Marrakesch (way geometries) stay local and are not tracked; `scripts/altstadt-umrisse.ts` reads them to regenerate the outlines. `messwerte.json` holds the measurement centers. The derived work `src/lib/catalog/data/altstaedte.ts` is shared under the same license (share-alike).
