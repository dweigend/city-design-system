# AI/ML for the Evolutionary City

As of 2026-11-12. Research and proposal, no implementation or performance measurement. Synthesis by Codex, parallel research by two subagents, independent counter-check by GPT-6 Astra. Basis: Research 100, 102, 105, and 107 as well as Agent Loom materials from the author's project. No author sign-off.

**ML pays off as a small proposal generator or fast surrogate for expensive calculations. City state should continue to arise from explicit, verifiable events.** Learning and deterministic execution are not mutually exclusive. For neural networks, however, a seed alone does not guarantee identical decisions across devices.

Particularly fitting is [Talus with its Model Card](https://huggingface.co/osfv/talus): a terrain diffusion model with 23 million parameters trained on its own procedural maps. This supports the idea of using our generators as a data source. It proves neither better hydrology nor better urban development. The rejected landscape attempts in Research 107 show precisely why a convincing image is not a sufficient criterion.

## Five Recommendations

1. **ML proposes; public actions decide.** Network inputs are existing stock, drivers, and neighborhood. Outputs are suitability scores or fully parameterized event candidates. Validator, ordering, IDs, and quantity balances remain explicit. Accepted actions along with parameters and versions are stored; replay needs no re-inference. This preserves alignment with "Evolution instead of plan" and the headless architecture.
2. **Start with small numerical networks.** A CNN/MLP with roughly 0.1–1 million parameters is a sensible search range for suitability fields. Only the task determines size. A [135M language model](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M) brings little relevant prior benefit for five numerical city actions. Synthetic data saves licensing and collection effort, but inherits the flaws of its generators. Mere imitation of cheap rules does not justify ML.
3. **Test terrain diffusion separately and offline first.** [Talus](https://huggingface.co/osfv/talus) and [InfiniteDiffusion](https://github.com/xandergos/terrain-diffusion) are concrete starting points. Sketch conditioning is evidenced by [terrain authoring research](https://pubs.cs.uct.ac.za/id/eprint/1583/). River, floodplain, and runoff are hard conditions: re-check after every relevant elevation change. Pure surface detail may alternatively alter representation only. Diffusion does not automatically replace hydrology or cheap noise.
4. **Explore macro dynamics as a forecasting model.** Encoder → latent transition under drivers → decoder could approximate consequences of flooding, influx, or policy. Initially score candidates only; do not overwrite city state. [ML surrogates for agent-based transportation simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11057) show the methodological link. Real historical data does not prove causal effects of new future policies.
5. **Treat browser budget and aesthetics as selection criteria.** [ONNX Runtime Web](https://onnxruntime.ai/docs/get-started/with-javascript/web.html) with WASM is the first export path, WebGPU an acceleration to be measured. Inference in a Worker and between epochs, not in the render loop. Three-dimensional forms remain derivable in three.js from structured data. A model should support the Bible aesthetic; today's car-centric cities are not a neutral training target for that.

## Counter-Check and Limits

The independent critic checked the recommendations for objective function, replay, and long-term behavior. Incorporated: rules **and simple candidate search** as baselines; hold out entire episodes and scenario families; measure rejections, stagnation, and use-change cycles; longer rollouts rather than single-step error alone. Quantization and argmax do not prove platform parity. The [PyTorch documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/randomness.html) explicitly warns about reproducibility limits.

Learned macro dynamics is not a proven market gap. Research shows adjacent work, not a complete market survey. [GameNGen](https://gamengen.github.io/) and Genie-like video worlds generate interactive images; they offer no proven replacement for durable building identities, event logs, and area balances.
