# City Rules

**The city retains, converts and extends, and it connects everything overhead.** Historic streets remain as inherited space for paths, supply and courtyards. They carry no cars.

## 1 Mechanics

### 1.1 Resource cycles

- **Component passport.** Each part has ID, recipe, provenance and condition. It is never installed and stored at once.
- **Stock balance.** 1000 kg deconstruction yields 700 kg reuse, 180 kg reprocessed, 120 kg loss and reject.
- **Simultaneity.** 12 kWh solar against 18 kWh demand gives 44 % self-sufficiency.
- **Sponge.** Two rains in a row overflow 88 m³ instead of 20 m³.

### 1.2 Building on existing stock

Retain → convert → extend. Layer limits: housing 5, commercial 4, production 3, park 1. Stock geometry stays fixed. A removed base raises a visible conflict.

### 1.3 Paths and elevated walkways

Bridges span 6–18 m and are 1.8–3 m wide. The network is a spanning tree plus 1–3 loops. A crossing at different heights is no connection. A service behind a barrier does not count.

### 1.4 Microcosm building

Each level has a program and a capacity: market → repair → housing → learning → garden → energy roof. Support stays; infill fills bays of 3–6 m.

## 3 History and image

### 3.1 Pre-grow, compile, events

The access graph, blocks and parcels grow before play. A checked carrier is compiled once. Later paths and splits are local events with provenance.

### 3.2 Stock record

Stable ID, predecessor, epoch, typology, frontage, protection and load capacity. Age is data; gray only displays it.

### 3.3 Style rules

- 75 % calm surface, 20 % repeated elements, 5 % accent.
- Color belongs to a legible part. A mode change alters encoding, never state.
- Vary form or surface, not both.
- A preview differs visibly from built work; a repaired spot stays recognizable.
- One emphasis channel per focus; at most two accent colors per group.
