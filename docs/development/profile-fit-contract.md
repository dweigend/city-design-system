# Forms that follow their surroundings

A fitting starts with its host. Resolve the saved socket, its local axes and the facing surface before choosing the size or position of the guest. Dimensions are in metres. The seed chooses a recipe within those constraints. Iteration order and camera zoom must never choose the recipe.

Context-sensitive rules in procedural architecture can read the geometry around a local construction frame. Here the frame belongs to a socket or bay and the constraints come from its actual wall or footprint. [Müller et al., Procedural Modeling of Buildings](https://www.peterwonka.net/Publications/pdfs/2006.SG.Mueller.ProceduralModelingOfBuildings.final.pdf)

## Three operations in the engine

- **Fit inside a bay.** Express the edges of an ordered convex footprint as outward planes around the anchor. For each plane, divide the available distance by the guest's support in that direction. Every non-negative bound limits the design scale, including zero. An anchor on an edge has zero admissible scale when the guest extends toward that edge. An outside anchor yields zero. Megastructure tanks and greenhouses use the four mounted corners, including in skewed bays.
- **Meet a neighbour.** Allocation of free space and physical contact are separate operations. Slide the fitting along the socket axis until the facing outlines touch. For a circular host on a common seat, its reach at height **z** is **√(z(2R − z))**. Add the guest's reach and maximize over each linear segment, checking its stationary point within the shared height interval. Keep the winning segment and fraction when reducing mesh detail. Two circular sections give the simpler separation **2√(Rr)**. Water fittings, solar rings and the box fittings share these rules. The deliberately inset socket seam is measured along the mounting axis. This construction applies collision-boundary reasoning to one constrained translation. [Lozano-Pérez, Spatial Planning](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/b9b90ab6-10d9-4934-8d7e-5eedd65680d6)
- **Follow a skin.** Define an annulus in a local tangent chart, evaluate its vertices on the host and give its front and back separate offsets. A tube capsule evaluates the cylindrical barrel and the rounded end in the same chart. Its portholes still have an open bore when the available frontage changes.

The headless implementation lives in `src/lib/catalog/logic/profile-fit`. The footprint operation assumes a convex site. The contact operation assumes a horizontal mounting direction and an upright profile. The surface chart must be single-valued over the entire trim and oriented toward the exterior. Installation ownership, inventory and structural validation remain the caller's responsibility.
