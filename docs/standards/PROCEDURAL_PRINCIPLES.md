# Procedural Principles

As of 2026-11-12. Draft; legend, source abbreviations, and status are in [README](README.md).

Owner for: determinism, units and coordinate systems, numerics, topology and embedding, projection, budgets, and measurement duty. Applies to all generators and rules under `src/lib/catalog/logic/` and their projection in `scene/`.

**Reading guide.** Every rule is formulated to be testable. The "Evidence" column states what is documented today. "open" means: the rule applies, its compliance has not been measured. No rule counts as satisfied merely by appearing in this file. Measurements belong to the linked reports; only references appear here. Tests from 95 were not re-run for this file.

Evidence: [01 · Universal Grid](../research/01-universal-grid.md), [02 · Geometry Principles](../research/02-geometry-principles.md). Further reviews, target criteria and measurement reports (KE, K3, GE, SG) live in the private design lab.

Five spaces. They are never silently mixed.

| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------- |
| Carrier  | `(quad, u, v, level)` with `u, v ∈ [0, 1]`                                              | dimensionless | Topology in `logic/`      |
| Grid     | Indices or IDs of a grid generator                                                      | integer       | respective generator only |
| World    | `x, y` ground plane; `z` height; right-handed; rotation around `z` positive CCW in plan | m, rad        | `logic/`                  |

| Screen   | CSS pixels; in 2D drawings `1 U = 4 px = 1 m`                                           | px            | Components, `src/app.css` |

## 3. Numerics

| ID    | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Source · Auth.                                                | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| PN-1  | **No global magic tolerance.** Every tolerance has a name, unit, reference measure, and operation. It is derived from scale, e.g., area sum `1e-9 · max(1, A)`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | SK (decisions); 95 §Executable Invariants 4 · N\*             | Current: many unnamed literals from `1e-6` to `1e-12` in `logic/` (search 2026-11-12). Clean up when touching code, no mass edits.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| PN-2  | **Robust predicates.** Topological decisions (orientation, side, intersection yes/no, inside/outside) use a floating-point filter and compute exact signs when uncertain. Constructions (intersection point, area) remain floating-point and are not called exact.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | 95 §Executable Invariants (`orient`); 96 G02, U7 · V          | `orient` in core (95). No stress test (KE E7).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| PN-3  | **One predicate, one owner.** Geometric predicates are never copied.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | ST §Duplication rule · D                                      | Open; no duplicate test.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| PN-4  | **Integer decisions.** Whatever must agree across runtimes decides on integers: quantized dimensions (`1 mm`) or integer hash. The quantization contract specifies per quantity: rounding rule including half-way behavior, value range, declared overflow handling (never silent, [PT-10](#4-topology-and-embedding)), and origin of the quantized input (stored authoritatively as integer or derived from Float64). Quanta derived from floats are only as consistent as their inputs. Boundary cases near half a unit and at range limits are compared across declared runtimes. Integers alone do not prove determinism. | 100 D6; 96 U10; Review · V                                    | KE E8 used cost in whole millimeters, under Bun only; that does not establish runtime parity. No quantization contract, no boundary-case comparison. Open.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| PN-5  | **Degeneracy is defined per operation.** Every operation declares which degeneracies it rejects, which it supports, and what happens to supported ones; likewise boundary membership (point on edge, interval endpoints). `NaN`, `Infinity`, and zero-length edges are rejected at the boundary with an error code. Collinearity is not a general error: supported collinear boundary vertices are preserved. If an operation requires strict convexity or distinct points, that lives in its contract and produces a declared error code, never silent repair.                                                               | 95 §Executable Invariants 1, 2, 5; 95 §Polygon → Quad · V     | Core: tests for bow-tie, self-intersection, poor quality (95). Collinear rings go through triangulation (95 §Polygon → Quad). Counter-probe 2026-11-12: `compile` and `validateModel` accept the ring `(0,0),(5,0),(10,0),(10,10),(0,10)`; zero edge, `NaN`, `Infinity`, and zero-area ring are rejected. Remaining generators open. |
| PN-6  | **Shared edges** are computed once from an ID in canonical order (sorted endpoint pair). Both neighbors receive bit-identical values.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | 95 §Spezifikation (IDs); 96 §7 · V                            | 95: seam identity across 80 affine convex polygons.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| PN-7  | **Topology never from rounded coordinates.** Connectivity comes from shared IDs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | 96 U5; SG Measurement contract · V                            | SG: rounding produced 7 instead of 68 blocks. The 1-mm noding there was an export fix, not a production contract.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| PN-8  | **Quality measures are an executable contract:** `J_s`, minimum angle, aspect ratio, area. Bounds are parameters and are never silently relaxed. Failing means error, not repair.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | 95 §Executable Invariants 5, §Polygon → Quad; 96 G05 · V      | 95 measurement: `contour` rejected with default bounds (`quality-unattainable`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| PN-9  | The corner test `J_s > 0` holds only for planar bilinear quads. For trilinear solids it is no proof; interior samples are spot checks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | 95 §Executable Invariants; 96 §7; K3 Measurement contract · V | —                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| PN-10 | **Predicates do not replace domain dimensions.** Minimum frontage, minimum area, and clear width are metric rules and are checked separately.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | 95 §Executable Invariants 5; KE E6; K3 e1 · V                 | Measured as counter-evidence: KE E6 frontage ratio 95.72 → 70.40%; K3 e1 clear width below target despite valid embedding.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

## 4. Topology and Embedding

| ID    | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Source · Auth.                                    | Evidence                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PT-1  | **Carrier and position layer.** Topology, embedding, and representation are separate contracts. Topology = IDs and incidence. Embedding = positions. A grid is a position layer on a carrier; other valences need another carrier.     | AR §Kernels; 96 U1; 100 Core thesis · N\*         | Current: three models side by side (`Patch`, `UniversalPatch`, `Model`; 100 §3.2). Architecture D from 100 is not signed off (KE). |
| PT-2  | **Topology contract** names: cell IDs, dimension, neighbors with ordering, boundary handling, weights, mapping to geometry.                                                                                                            | AR §Topology as an extension boundary · N         | Open per grid.                                                                                                                     |
| PT-3  | **Grid-agnostic.** General algorithms read only the graph: no `i/j` arithmetic, no hidden square offset. Every algorithm names the grids on which it is tested. Quad-specific assumptions live in the contract.                        | AR §Topology; 95 Guiding goal · N                 | 95: ten grid families checked for topology and seam only with relaxed import bounds. 100 E7 (10 of 10) open.                       |
| PT-4  | **IDs** are stable and come from input and rule path, never from coordinates. Identity, index, and label are distinct. Split and merge require explicit mapping.                                                                       | 95 §Spezifikation (IDs); 96 U5; NC §Objects · N\* | 95: origin region keeps its ID; child IDs may change under repair.                                                                 |
| PT-5  | **Invariants.** Every operation declares its invariants and preserves them: finite coordinates, simple CCW rings, consistent half-edges (`next`, `prev`, `twin`), at most two faces per edge, provenance tree, area sum, quad quality. | 95 §Executable Invariants; 96 U7 · V              | Core tests in 95 (as of 2026-11-11).                                                                                               |
| PT-6  | Every operation declares what it checks locally and globally, and its cost. Locality is not claimed when a global check is performed.                                                                                                  | 95 §Local Changes, §Complexity; 99 No. 4 · V      | 95: local derivation, global overlap check.                                                                                        |
| PT-7  | **Transaction:** proposal → dependency closure → validation → commit. Atomic: if validation fails, the predecessor remains unchanged. A no-op returns the previous state. Shared, untouched structures are declared.                   | 95 §Local Changes; 96 U7, U8; 103 · V             | 95: property tests. Tests for sharing and no-op identity are missing (103 step 1).                                                 |
| PT-8  | `readonly` is not deep protection. The contract states whether an output is detached, a reference snapshot, or a live view ([C-23](CODING_STANDARDS.md#types-and-contracts)).                                                          | AR §Dependency and public-contract rules · N      | Current: `history(initial)` retains the input reference (103).                                                                     |
| PT-9  | **Undo/Redo** returns the exact saved states. Foreign or stale histories are rejected.                                                                                                                                                 | 95 §Local Changes · V                             | Core tests (95).                                                                                                                   |
| PT-10 | **Errors are results:** declared codes such as `quality-unattainable`, `landlocked`, `protected`. No silent repair, no silently lost area. Whether exception or result type is specified in the contract.                              | 95 §Polygon → Quad; 100 §2.1, §3.4 · V            | Current: exceptions (`InvariantError`). Form undecided.                                                                            |
| PT-11 | **Continuous and discrete separated.** Continuous: positions, heights, split ratios. Discrete: topology, occupancy. In the continuous regime no ID and no topology changes.                                                            | 100 §1.3 (G3); 96 §7 · V                          | KE E2, E6: 0 changes in the test domains.                                                                                          |
| PT-12 | **Morph only within verified bounds:** `J_s`, step size, metric minimum frontage. Outside them follows a declared event with hysteresis, no silent re-identification. Interpolation does not make an invalid target valid.             | 100 G1–G6; KE "Consequence for D" · V             | KE E2: unconstrained G1 failed; 14 of 20 lobed targets invalid. G4, G5 untested.                                                   |
| PT-13 | **Carrier coordinates preserve identity, not meter dimensions.** After every position-layer change, access and dimension rules are re-checked in meters.                                                                               | KE E6; K3 e1 · V                                  | Measured (see PN-10).                                                                                                              |
| PT-14 | **Caches** have owner, key, and invalidation. No cache outlives the topology it represents. Caches are instance-bound.                                                                                                                 | AR §Topology; 103 · N                             | Current deviation: module-wide cache in `welt/index.ts` (103 step 3).                                                              |
